## Why

The maintained Codex benchmark now blocks broad comparison execution until a diagnostic completes, but its 12-session diagnostic still spends on a behavioral matrix to answer a narrower infrastructure question. One controlled live canary is sufficient to prove that Codex starts with the selected onboarding context, retains complete evidence, and can be graded locally before a maintainer decides whether comparative evidence is worth further cost.

## What Changes

- Reduce the maintained live diagnostic to exactly one representative task, one active AIBoarding condition, and one repetition.
- Pin that canary to a low-cost, bounded Codex runtime profile and preserve complete JSONL evidence for independent local grading.
- Treat complete objective pass and fail as viable infrastructure outcomes while any incomplete evidence continues to block expansion.
- Keep broader comparisons behind a separate explicit maintainer invocation and preserve historical diagnostic evidence without mutation or publication.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `agent-outcome-benchmarking`: Make the maintained cost-bearing diagnostic a single controlled live canary while preserving the explicit comparison-expansion gate.

## Impact

Touches the maintained agent-outcome profile and Codex invocation controls in `benchmarks/agent-outcomes/`, deterministic tests in `tests/benchmarks/`, and the manual protocol in `docs/VERIFICATION.md`. It adds no authenticated test execution, new task unless reuse proves impossible, retry mechanism, benchmark framework, pricing integration, or publication behavior.
